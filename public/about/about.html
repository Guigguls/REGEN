About Us

Who We Are

REGEN is a student‑led initiative dedicated to building an AI‑powered waste management application. Our team combines technology and sustainability to create practical solutions that encourage responsible waste disposal.

Our Mission

We aim to empower individuals and communities to reduce their environmental impact by making waste classification simple, accessible, and engaging. Every small action contributes to a cleaner, greener future.

What We Do

REGEN uses AI image recognition to identify waste materials and provide proper disposal instructions. The app also tracks user activity, estimates carbon footprints, and motivates eco‑friendly habits through gamification features such as points, leaderboards, and digital certificates.

Our Vision

We envision REGEN becoming a trusted platform that helps communities adopt sustainable practices in everyday life. By combining technology with environmental awareness, we strive to make waste management easier, more engaging, and widely accessible.
